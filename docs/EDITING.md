# Updating the Ruslitiki page

After the maintainer has installed the editor once, double-click **Ruslitiki Editor.app** in the project folder. It opens the editor in your browser and runs quietly in the background. Save your draft before closing the browser; you can reopen it later. The app must stay with its project folder. The maintainer should check this launcher on Olga's Mac during setup; the native app launcher has not yet been exercised here.

1. Start in **Edit page**. Click text on the page and type. Select a section to see its controls, or use the settings on the left.
2. Drag a section using its **Move** handle. Drag a new block from the content palette onto the page, or press **+ Add section**, **Add before**, **Add after**, or **Add section here**. The page updates while you work. Press **Save draft** to keep your changes on this laptop.
3. Press **Prepare preview**. This saves your changes, checks the page and builds a separate **Saved preview**. Use **Phone**, **Tablet** or **Desktop**, or **Open preview** to see it in a separate tab. Saved preview links navigate normally. On the editing page, click menu words to edit them; use the adjacent arrow to jump to that section.
4. After the one-time publishing connection, press **Publish website** and confirm. Wait for the editor to say **Published**. A saved draft and a preview are not public updates.

The public coming-soon page stays available while you edit. If you change a draft after previewing it, prepare another preview before publishing. If another window has changed your saved draft, the editor asks you to reload rather than overwriting it.

After setup, **Publish website** sends your reviewed page to GitHub, and GitHub Actions updates the public website automatically. You do not need to open GitHub or run commands for routine updates. Use **Check publishing connection** during the one-time setup: it shows any owner settings still needed, with links, and enables publication when ready. Checking the connection preserves your draft and does not publish it. Until setup is complete, saving and previewing remain available.

**Text formatting:** click a passage, select words and use **Bold** or **Italic** in the floating toolbar. In paragraph text, use **Bullets** or **Numbered list**, and press Enter for a new paragraph. Headings, menu words, button labels and captions support inline bold and italic only. **Clear formatting** removes formatting from the selection. Changing a passage through its plain settings field replaces that field's formatting; use the page toolbar to retain formatting while editing. Save and prepare a preview to review the result.

**Labels & menu:** change **Our first book**, **by**, date labels, the membership label, signup button text, **Home**, **October's Book**, footer words and illustration captions here. Most visible labels can also be clicked and edited directly on the page. This panel also holds menu-button wording, accessibility descriptions, and the Privacy and Page not found copy. Other section menu labels live in **Page blocks**. Changing a label changes its wording; dates and destinations are edited separately.

**Dates & links:** click a displayed date in **Edit page** to open its calendar field here. Choose the date in the picker; if the calendar does not open automatically, use the focused date field's calendar control. Membership opening and reading start stack beneath the signup button, with the price alongside them or below on narrow screens. Leave the email empty until the mailbox works. Only switch from Coming soon when the real Patreon link is available; dates never activate the Patreon button automatically.

**AI-readable overview:** the website's `llms.txt` updates automatically when you prepare and publish the page. It uses the same club details, dates, visible sections and active links. Hidden sections stay out, and there is no separate file for you to edit.

**Images:** use a PNG, JPEG or WebP under 2 MB. New uploads stay in the editor's private working area. Only the selected logo and images in visible blocks are copied from that private area into the reviewed release. Previously published images may remain in the site's image library. Use the original wordmark button to return to the existing branding.

**Book illustration:** under **First book**, use **Show the Eugene Onegin illustration** to show or hide the historical garden scene. Edit its caption and credit directly on the page or in **Labels & menu**; its image description is also in that panel. Keep the artist credit accurate. Changing the book title or author hides this Onegin-specific artwork automatically.

**Background book drawings:** under **Layout & style**, turn **Background book drawings** on or off. Five faint book imprints sit in the wider layout’s open spaces. Smaller screens keep the header and footer accents; the other drawings appear only where there is room. Their tint follows your page colours. See [artwork details](BOOK_IMPRINTS.md).

**Tagline and Instagram:** the club description is centered beneath the wordmark, followed by the host's linked handle in parentheses. Click the description to edit it. Change **Host Instagram profile** under Introduction, or click the handle to open its settings; leave it empty to hide it. The footer links to the club's own Instagram. Click that handle to change its destination in **Dates & links**, or edit its preceding label through **Labels & menu**.

**Signup price:** edit **Membership price** under **Dates & links**, or click the price on the canvas. It appears to the right of the stacked signup dates, or below them on narrow screens; leave it empty to hide it. The Membership section and pricing FAQ contain separate text, so keep those consistent when changing prices. The current standard price is $15 USD/month.

**Club introduction video:** the **Meet Ruslitiki** block is enabled with Olga’s introduction video (`m9CKv9oMYRY`), after the opening and before How the club works. Its centered player scales at 16:9 up to 896 × 504 pixels at the default text size. To replace it, paste a new share or watch link into **YouTube video link** under **Page blocks** and prepare a preview. Check that the video plays before publishing. You can change its heading, introduction, width and position like any other section. The player uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed and does not autoplay. In **Edit page**, clicking the video opens its settings; use **Saved preview** to play it. You can add further video blocks from the palette.

**Frequently asked questions:** click a question or answer to edit it, or use **Page blocks**. Drag to reorder, duplicate an answer, or add **Question and answer** from the palette. Visitors expand answers individually; the list has one separator above the first question. The FAQ heading can stand alone without introductory text. Keep the first-book, dates and pricing answers consistent with the rest of the page. Add session times, replay arrangements and early-bird conditions when confirmed.

**Layout & style:** choose an introduction beside the book, the book on the left, one column, or a centred composition. Change page width, section spacing, wordmark size, heading size, paragraph size and button shape. Choose Literata or Golos Text independently for headings and paragraphs. Apply a colour palette or choose your own background, text and accent colours; unreadable combinations must be corrected before saving. Move the Onegin illustration above or below its title/description and change its size.

**FAQ motion:** answers gently slide open and fade in on the public page and Saved preview. They stay open in Edit page so you can work on their text. Visitors who prefer reduced motion get immediate opening and closing.

**Screen sizes:** Phone uses up to 390px, Tablet up to 768px, and Desktop uses the available preview width. These views resize both the editing page and Saved preview; use **Open preview** for more room. Below 900px the page stacks its columns and replaces the desktop menu with a three-line button that stays at the top right while you scroll. Its small background keeps the icon legible over page content, and section links leave room below it. Tap it to open the drawer from the right; use its close button to return to the same reading position. The closed mobile menu has no visible label or surrounding rules; one line below the title and tagline separates them from the introduction. Check both the menu and page in Phone view before publishing.

**Section menu:** in **Edit page**, click a menu label to edit its words and use the adjacent **↗** arrow to jump without losing unsaved edits. The Home arrow scrolls to the top. In **Saved preview**, labels navigate normally and Home opens the root homepage. These controls work in the desktop bar and mobile drawer.

The menu follows your block order and leaves out hidden or removed sections. Use **Labels & menu** for Home and the first-book link; use each block's **Menu label** to rename, add or remove other links. Clear a block's label to leave it out. When the video immediately precedes its explanation, **How it works** starts at the video. Moving or hiding the video returns that link to the explanation; giving the video its own label creates a separate link. Editing How it works changes the explanation's menu label even when its arrow starts at the video.

**Page blocks:** add up to 24 text, question-and-answer, image-and-text, YouTube video, button, or quote blocks. Drag them directly on the page or in the page-order list; arrow buttons provide another way to move them. Blocks can go before or after the introduction and first book, which move together. The wordmark and footer stay in their places. Use **Options** on the page to open a block's settings, **Duplicate** to reuse it, **Hide** to keep it off the page, or **Remove** to delete it from the draft. Hidden blocks remain available under **Page blocks**.

**Section links:** open a block's **Options** or expand it under **Page blocks**, then edit **Section link**. Enter a short name such as `contact` or `reading-calendar`, without `#`; use lowercase letters, numbers and single hyphens, starting with a letter. The Contact information link is `#contact`. New sections get a readable link from their heading (or button label) when saved. Once saved, changing the heading or moving the section keeps its link. Duplicate sections get their own link. The menu and `llms.txt` use the chosen link automatically; the original automatic link continues to work for existing bookmarks. Changing a custom link later changes its shareable address, so update any buttons or messages that used that previous custom link. Link names must be unique, including hidden sections. Leave the field blank to restore the default link, then save and prepare a preview.

**Buttons:** add a standalone button block or a button inside any other block. Links to other websites and the privacy page open in a new tab; Home and section links stay in the current tab, while email links use the mail application. Edit its words on the page, then set its destination in the settings. Choose filled, outline or text styling. A button needs both a label and a safe destination before previewing or publishing.

Each block has width, alignment and plain/bordered/accent-background choices. Text blocks offer one column, two text columns, or a heading beside the text. Image blocks offer left/right/above placement and original/landscape/square/portrait proportions, plus an image description, editable caption and source credit link. Use images you have permission to publish. Layouts adapt automatically to smaller screens.

**Section separators:** open a block's **Options** on the page, or expand it under **Page blocks**, and choose **Separator above**. **Show line** adds a horizontal line above the section; **No line** removes it. **Automatic** keeps the existing video/introduction and FAQ grouping, and adds a line before the section following the FAQs, such as Contact information. New text, image, video and quote blocks start with a line; new questions and buttons stay grouped automatically. The line follows the block's width and alignment. A bordered background still keeps its surrounding box. Save, prepare a preview, and publish as usual.

**Unfinished work:** new blocks can be saved privately before their text, image or button is complete. The editor asks you to finish visible blocks before preparing a publishable preview. Each open tab has its own unsaved editing page; saving still checks for changes made in another tab.

**Undo and reset:** Undo/Redo keeps your last 40 changes in the current editor tab, including removed blocks and style changes. Typing in one passage is grouped into one undo step. This history clears when you reload the tab. **Reset page style** restores the original visual settings while keeping your content and block order. **Edit page** shows changes as you work; prepare a new **Saved preview** before publishing them.

**Recovery:** after there are two confirmed publications, Restore previous version copies the earlier content into a draft for review. Your newer draft is backed up first. Review and publish the restoration when ready. It does not silently change the live page.

If publication reports that the host has not confirmed a release, do not assume it failed or succeeded. The editor retains the attempted version so the same update can be checked again. Ask the maintainer to check the hosting connection if this persists.

This editor has no recurring editor subscription and works on the configured laptop. Drafts do not yet sync between computers. Back up the whole project including its hidden `.studio` folder. Private drafts and backups must not be copied into a public repository.

**Receiving an editor update:** a GitHub push or browser refresh does not install new editor code on another laptop. The maintainer must update that laptop's project and restart the editor process after you save your draft. Closing the browser alone is not enough: the app can reopen an older process still running in the background. On its first start, this contact update backs up the saved draft, adds Contact information if missing, and replaces the old contact address with **olga@ruslitiki.com** where applicable. It preserves your other text, formatting and layout choices. Open **Page blocks → Contact information → Separator above** to check the line, then prepare a fresh preview. Later intentional edits are not reapplied or reset by this one-time update.

The local editor is the supported editing workflow. The earlier `.pages.yml` hosted CMS configuration is retained as a reference; it is not activated and does not cover the expanded layout/block model. It needs updating and its own publishing design before use. Hosting settings and executable HTML/CSS remain maintainer responsibilities.
